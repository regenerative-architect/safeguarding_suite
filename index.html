Doctrine & provenance

Zero-Harm / Anti-Inversion note

Safeguarding tools can themselves become harmful if they reward suspicion, surveillance, stigma, coercion or unreviewable authority. This bundle is designed to resist that inversion.

Guardrails

  • No person-level danger score.
  • No automated guilt determination.
  • No hidden telemetry.
  • No external transfer without an explicit user action.
  • No gamification for accusation volume or punishment.
  • Evidence, allegation, inference and simulation stay separate.
  • Appeal, correction, repair and data portability are first-class.
  • Accessibility and degraded-mode behavior are explicit.
